Recipe

Soft and chewy choclate cookies that are simple to make and great. Acording to Sally McKenny this recipe is the most popular on her site and has been on there since 2013. Typically produces anywehre from 16-20 cookies depending on size.

Tips

The recipe takes about 30 minutes to make and bake, 2 hours and 45 minutes including the minium time to chill the dough. The dough can be prepped up to three days prior, check it out and comeback then if you wish to do so. The dough can and works well when chilled overnight. Roll the dough extra taller

Ingredients needed

    Key ingredients

  • Melted Butter (Melted then cooled for 5 minutes), ¾ (170g/12Tbsp)
  • Brown sugar, ¾ (150g)
  • White sugar, ½ (100g)
  • Conrstarch, 1½
  • Egg and Egg yolk (room temperature), 1

    • Other ingredients

  • Pure vanilla extract, 2 teaspoons
  • Baking soda, 1 teaspoon
  • All-purpose Flour, 2¼ cups (281g)
  • Salt, ½ teaspoon
  • Chocolate Chips of choice, 1¼ (225g)

Recipe steps

    • With a large bowl whisk flour, baking soda, cornstarch, and salt, then set aside for now.

    • With a medium bowl, whisk together the melted butter, brown sugar, and white sugar until no lumps are left.

    • Then whisk in the egg and egg yolk until it combines.

    • Repeat with vanilla extract, the mixture should be thin

    • Pour into the large bowl set asied earlier, mix thoroughly until the the dough is soft, thick, and shiny.

    • Fold in the choice of choclate, it may not stick as of now, try you best to make it do so


  2. Cover the dough tightly and refrigerate anywhere between 2 hours or 3 days, doing so overnight is highy recommended


  3. When ready, preheat oven to 325℉, line a large baking sheet with baking mats, if chilled for over 2 hours let the dough chill for around 15 minutes


    • Scoop about 3 scant tablespoons (2 ounces/60g) for XL cookies, or 2 heaping tablespoons (1.75 ounces/50g) for medium.

    • Roll the clumps into balls, shape the cooki dough more like a cylinder than a circle, taller rather than wider. Allows for thicker cookies.

    • Arrange cookies 3 inches apart on the baking sheets.


  5. Bake for 13 or 14 minutes, or until edges are lightly browned, then leave them to cool for about 10 minutes

  6. Enjoy!
