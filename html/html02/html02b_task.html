Ultimate Party Planning Guide

Step-by-step instructions and checklists for a perfect event

Planning Your Party

Timeline

  1. Choose a date and venue (4-6 weeks ahead)
  2. Create and send invitations (3 weeks ahead)
  3. Plan the menu and purchase ingredients (1 week ahead)
  4. Decorate and set up (1 day before)
  5. Enjoy the party! (Day of)

Guest Essentials

  • Comfortable seating
  • Good lighting
  • Beverage station
  • Music or entertainment
  • Clean restrooms

Food Preparation

  1. Prepare appetizers
    • Arrange on platters
    • Chill until serving
  2. Cook main dishes
    • Set oven temperature
    • Monitor cooking times
  3. Prepare desserts
    • Bake ahead is possible
    • Keep refrigerated
  4. Set up the drink station

Party Supplies Checklist

  • Plates
    • Paper or ceramis
    1 per guest, and extras
  • Cups
    • 2-3 per guest
  • Napkins
    • 4-5 per guest
  • Utensils
    • Forks, spoons, knives for each guest
  • Trash bags
    • 3-4 large bags for clean up
