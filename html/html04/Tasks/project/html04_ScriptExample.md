# Lesson 04: Example Promo Scripts

Two examples of scripts are here, plus a blank template. Do not copy these, your script has to be about your own site.

Remember the limits: about 45 words, two lego characters max, say the site name at the end, and it has to time out at 17 seconds or under when you read it out loud.

## Blank Template

Fill this in on paper first. Time it out loud with your partner before you show it to Mr. McMaster.

| Seconds| Who       | Line                                                  | What's on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4    | Guy       | Augh! This is so frustrating!                         | Guy is sitting at a computer typing |
| 4–9    | Other guy | Whats wrong? And why are you yelling at the computer? | Other guy walks in concerned to hear Guy yelling |
| 9-15   | Guy       | Im trying to design a website! But html is so hard!   | Guy turns around complaining to Other guy |
| 15–17  | Other guy | Then go to Htm-learning and learn more html!          | Other guy is speaking like he is sponsored wrapping up the video |

**Word count:** 35 (aim for about 45)

**Timed reading:** ~15 seconds (has to be 17 or under)

### Blank shot list

1. Start with Guy at the computer
2. Cut to Other guy walking in a door frame
3. Cut back to Guy at the desk
4. Frame remains, Other guy walks onto screen
5. Cut to the computer screen which now has Htm-learning's logo on it
